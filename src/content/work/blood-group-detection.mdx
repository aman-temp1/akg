## Problem

Knowing a blood group usually means drawing blood. Fingerprint ridge patterns are known to correlate with blood group, which opens the door to a fast, non-invasive first screen.

## Approach

- An **ensemble of CNN, ResNet50 and VGG16** models, combined to be more robust than any single network.
- A **preprocessing pipeline** with heavy augmentation (25% shear and rotation) so the model tolerates how people actually press their fingers on a scanner.
- Validation on **6,000+ clinical fingerprint samples**.

## Delivery

The model is packaged in Docker and served through a Flask web app, so a clinician can upload a fingerprint and get a prediction in the browser.

## Results

The ensemble reached **92.2% accuracy** in blood group classification, giving a credible non-invasive screen that can reduce the number of blood tests needed.
