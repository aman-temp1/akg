## Problem

Students at KIIT juggle notes in group chats, old exam papers in random drives, and section swaps negotiated one message at a time. There was no single place to find what you need for a semester.

## Approach

I built KIIT Hub as a one-stop academic platform and shipped it end to end: product, interface, backend, payments and hosting.

- **Study notes repository** organised by course and semester.
- **Previous year papers** with solutions.
- **Faculty ratings** driven by the community.
- **Section swapping** that automatically matches students whose preferences complement each other.
- **SGPA calculator** with course-specific grading parameters, and a **Pomodoro timer** for focused sessions.

## Architecture

The frontend is a React and TypeScript app built with Vite, with client-side routing and a responsive layout for every device. Supabase provides authentication and a PostgreSQL database secured with row-level security. Cloudflare handles hosting and the CDN, Bunny.net serves heavy study material, and Razorpay processes payments for premium features.

## Results

KIIT Hub gained more than 800 active users within a month of launch. It became a self-sustaining ecosystem where students contribute material, rate faculty and help each other swap sections. Next on the roadmap are a discussion forum, personalised study plans and live updates from university systems.
