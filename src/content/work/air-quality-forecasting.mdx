## Problem

Air quality sensors produce noisy, gappy data, yet people need to know what the air will be like in the next few hours, not just right now.

## Approach

- **Automated cleaning** with outlier detection and imputation from moving averages and seasonal decomposition.
- **Time series decomposition** to separate trend from daily, weekly and annual cycles.
- A **Prophet model** with custom seasonality for each of those cycles, forecasting CO, NOx and relative humidity.
- **Diagnostics** with cross-validation and interactive plots of trend components and forecast uncertainty.

## Results

The pipeline produces hourly forecasts of key air quality indicators at 92% accuracy, with uncertainty bands that make it clear how much to trust each prediction.
