## Overview

The dataset was missing 17% of item weights and 28% of store sizes, so the pipeline starts with mean and mode imputation and IQR-based outlier capping. Eight categorical variables are label-encoded, and a tuned XGBoost regressor predicts sales. Feature importance analysis shows which product and store attributes drive revenue, which feeds inventory decisions.
