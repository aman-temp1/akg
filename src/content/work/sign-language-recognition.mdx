## Problem

Most people don't know sign language, and that gap makes everyday conversations hard for people who rely on it. The goal was a tool that runs on an ordinary webcam and translates gestures as they happen.

## Approach

- **MediaPipe Hands** extracts 21 landmarks per hand from each frame.
- A **custom angle-based classifier** computes the angles between key joints, labels each finger as extended or folded, and matches the geometry against known gestures.
- **Temporal smoothing** removes flicker between frames.
- A **Flask backend with WebSockets** streams video and results to a responsive web interface, with a timestamped history that exports to TXT or CSV.

## Challenges

Lighting changes broke early versions, so I added adaptive brightness normalisation and histogram equalisation. To hold 30 FPS on low-end machines I tuned the MediaPipe configuration and skip frames when the device falls behind.

## Results

The system recognises 15+ ASL gestures, including letters, numbers and symbols such as "OK" and "Peace", at 95% accuracy in controlled conditions with under 50 ms of latency. It is deployed on AWS EC2.
